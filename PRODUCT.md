# Product

## Register

pretigious

## Users

Founders, operators, and growth leaders evaluating Pretigious for professional web development, AI automation, digital marketing, social media handling, and custom digital systems. They arrive with business pressure, scattered channels, and a need to trust the team before they start a project.

## Product Purpose

The site sells the agency's taste, engineering discipline, and operating maturity. Success means a visitor understands Pretigious can design distinctive public experiences while also building the behind-the-scenes systems that keep ads, approvals, automation, social publishing, and reporting under control.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, and controlled. The voice should feel expert and quietly ambitious, with enough visual risk to prove the agency has taste. The visual identity leans into black, red, maroon, and orange glow with sharp agency typography and presentation-grade motion.

## Anti-references

Avoid YouTube-tutorial aesthetics, generic agency cards, repeated section labels, decorative icons without purpose, overused gold luxury styling, cold blue/purple AI gradients, and normal brochure layouts that could belong to any small web studio.

## Design Principles

1. Show command through composition, not clutter.
2. Let imagery, motion, and layout prove the agency's capability before the copy asks for trust.
3. Make operational depth visible: approvals, dashboards, automation health, and reporting should feel designed, not bolted on.
4. Use glass and light as physical materials with restraint, never as a decorative preset.
5. Build for confidence on every viewport: readable type, generous spacing, clear actions, and reduced-motion support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic headings, form labels, and reduced-motion alternatives for scroll and entrance animation. Avoid color-only status communication.
